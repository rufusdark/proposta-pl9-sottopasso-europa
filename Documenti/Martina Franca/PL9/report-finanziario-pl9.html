Infrastructure Project PL9

Sottopasso Rotatoria Europa

Financial Report FY 2026
Status: Final Proposal

Executive Summary

The PL9 "Sottopasso Rotatoria Europa" is a critical urban infrastructure project designed to alleviate traffic congestion at the Europa roundabout. By constructing a dedicated underpass, the project aims to reduce transit times by 40% and improve local air quality. The total investment of €2.5M represents a strategic allocation of municipal funds towards long-term urban efficiency and safety.
